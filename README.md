### About Me

ML **researcher** and engineer (MSc AI, First Class Hons) working on **efficient deep learning** KV-cache compression, quantization and efficient LLM inference.
I care about the research questions *and* whether the answer survives contact with real hardware.

Day job: perception & autonomy at **Syos Aerospace** real-time models on Jetson / TensorRT in demanding production environments. Alongside that I'm going deeper into research on efficient inference and contributing across the open-source ML ecosystem.

What I care about: models that work outside the datacenter, honest benchmarking on real hardware and reproducible research.
